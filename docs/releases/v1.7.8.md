# NHL Tracker v1.7.8

## Trio strategy comparisons
- Compare original 1u trios with three 0.5u pairs, or use equal-exposure comparisons.
- See cumulative returns, strategy win counts, and a historical split/keep optimizer.
- Pregame trios remain provisional and update with saved lineup observations.
- Each game's trios lock when NHL status indicates puck drop: confirmed lines are preferred, otherwise saved projections are used.
- Enter reference odds during intermissions or after the games without changing locked players.
- Remove individual trios from comparison and restore them later.
- Game IDs and NHL slate dates are validated; wrong-date duplicate entries cannot enter comparison totals.
- Only locked trios with complete prices and settled outcomes count toward financial comparisons and the optimizer. Missing historical pregame lineups are not reconstructed from current lines.

## Confidence data and history
- Check + Recalculate + Record refreshes sources, recalculates scores, and records eligible pregame values.
- Startup and scheduled source checks, clearer logging status, source timestamps, and coverage warnings.
- Refreshed NHL goals/shots/PP/PK inputs; all-situations expected goals and high-danger chances per game.
- Season team history, multi-team game plots, confidence/profit quadrants, and the 72.5 confidence divider.
- Analysis begins October 21; unavailable scores and prices remain disclosed.
- Matchup confidence remains the average of offense /100 and opposing defensive vulnerability /100. Starting goalies are informational and do not contribute to this release's confidence score.
- Existing sizing thresholds remain: boost at 85+ (1.5x), normal at 72.5–<85 (1x), fade at 67.5–<72.5 (0.5x), skip below 67.5.

## Login and goal cards
- Optional Stay logged in on this computer securely encrypts remembered credentials and signs you back in on startup.
- Logging out clears the remembered login. TEST and LIVE logins stay separate.
- New goal artwork for all 32 teams, with team-colored borders.
- Removed the unnecessary event/goal sequence number from goal cards.

## Install
Close NHL Tracker, download the Windows x64 installer below, and install v1.7.8. Sign into your LIVE account; check Stay logged in if desired.

Automatic lineup collection requires the app running pregame. Reopening can recover saved pregame observations, but cannot reconstruct changes the app never observed. Account and NHL data services require an internet connection. Threshold optimizers are historical research tools and do not change wagers automatically.
