# NHL Tracker v1.7.7

Updated October 21, 2026. This installer replaces the earlier 1.7.7 report-fix build.

## Bet Entry
- Two-step bet slip: build and confirm legs, then complete sportsbook, units and pricing.
- More space for player search and two columns of slip legs, with larger quantity and stat controls.
- Quick Bet All Combos supports confirmed only or confirmed plus projected lineups.
- Trio prompts offer keeping the trio or splitting it into three pairs, including bulk entry.
- Default sportsbook and split-pair unit settings fill new wagers and remain editable.
- Clear confirmed and projected lineup indicators.

## Scoreboard and settings
- Updated team crests, including Los Angeles.
- Team shots on goal beneath each logo/abbreviation; game time moves lower within the compact card.
- Short intermission labels and EN indicators for pulled goalies.
- Cleaner Settings and cloud import layout.
- Historical feed loading is silent; notification settings apply to newly detected live events.

## Confidence analytics and automatic recording
- Season COMBOS P/L compared with confidence-adjusted P/L.
- Multi-team, per-game scatter plot with L1/L5/L10/Season filters and 70-confidence/0u reference lines.
- Read-only historical threshold optimizer and offensive/defensive score history.
- Strategy: BOOST >=85 at 1.5x; NEUTRAL 72.5–<85 at 1x; FADE 67.5–<72.5 at 0.5x; SKIP <67.5.
- Automatic pregame combo snapshots: confirmed lineups replace projections before puck drop; never-confirmed projections remain eligible.
- Automatic final-boxscore grading without generating daily PDFs. Failed requests retry; missing player evidence remains unverified.
- Eligible COMBOS results refresh combo profitability and last-five factors before daily score recording. Other model inputs keep their original dates.
- Missing scores and prices are disclosed, never fabricated. Actual wagers and historical predictions are not rewritten.

Automatic collection requires the app running pregame. Saved games are checked after reopening. Shared server-side event collection is not included in this release.

## Install
Close NHL Tracker, download the Windows installer below, install, and sign back into your LIVE account. If you installed the earlier 1.7.7 build, download this replacement directly; the version label remains 1.7.7.
